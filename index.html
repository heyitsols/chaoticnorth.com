04 / Where to start

Put a critical service to the test.

Start with a resilience exercise built around a failure that matters to your organisation. We examine how the technical response, decision-making and communications work together under pressure.

  1. 01Choose the service, failure scenario and people who need to be in the room.
  2. 02Work through the incident together, from the first signal to recovery.
  3. 03Identify the gaps, agree the most useful fixes and plan how to retest them.

The output: a clear record of what happened in the exercise, the dependencies it exposed and a prioritised action plan.
